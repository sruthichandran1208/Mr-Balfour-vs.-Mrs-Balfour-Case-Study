# Mr-Balfour-vs.-Mrs-Balfour-Case-Study

### Balfour v. Balfour – Case Study

This case study is about the **Balfour v. Balfour** case and its importance in contract law. It explains the facts of the case, the issue between Mr. and Mrs. Balfour, and the court’s decision. This case helps us understand that a promise between family members is not always treated as a legal contract.
